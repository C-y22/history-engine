Figure 4 · Activation test (H2b)

The formula reads the price: latent power before the event predicts the offshore price shock after it

Each point is one controlled commodity. X-axis = its latent power measured before the cutoff of its own exam — criticality × unpreparedness, the same product specification used throughout, fixed by theory before the test was run. Y-axis = offshore price change in roughly the month after the announcement. Prices are the formula's home domain: a shock needs both a real chokehold on supply and a buyer who has not stockpiled.
● hard point (quoted / assessed price)○ descriptive or estimated▫ window mismatch (sensitivity only)
Statistics: Spearman ρ = 0.89 (n=10, one-sided permutation p = 0.0008) under the product specification. The same ten points give ρ = 0.30 (not significant) under unpreparedness alone and ρ = 0.82 under share alone — the single-factor rulers, one of which was mis-specified in an earlier draft; all three are reported (Figure 5). Over the longest available window the product specification gives ρ = 0.80 (n=12, p = 0.002).
The group that is off the chart (itself evidence): the deepest-latent controlled commodities — samarium, gadolinium, lutetium (2025-04) and holmium, erbium, thulium, europium, ytterbium (2025-10) — have no active offshore quotation at all (Fastmarkets only began publishing European prices for some heavy rare earths in May–August 2025). They were too dark even to have a thermometer, so they cannot be plotted. Yttrium oxide has no one-month point; by November 2025 it stood +4,400% against the start of the year. Both facts run with the hypothesis, not against it.
Measurement basis: offshore markets (Rotterdam / Europe / US). Bismuth is a conservative lower bound 2.5 weeks after the announcement (+100–200% by 21 Feb; recorded as +100%). Natural graphite is FOB China (no independent offshore benchmark). Indium (+0.6–2.0%), tellurium (+7.2%) and molybdenum (+1.0%) are two-point hard readings from archived snapshots (SMI Europe retail / DailyMetalPrice; snapshot dates and redirects verified). For dysprosium and terbium the archived retail-metal snapshots (+35–37% over one month) agree in direction with the SMM oxide basis (×2–3). Tungsten (+18% over three months) is a window mismatch and enters sensitivity only.
Licensed-data corroboration (Benchmark Mineral Intelligence, Q1 2026): two hard confirmations at the quiet end — exam B, graphite: FOB China quarterly prices Q3→Q4 2023 fell, spherical −4.5% and flake −0.6%, in the quarter of the control; exam E2, anode / synthetic graphite: DDP China quarterly prices Q3→Q4 2025 rose only 1–3%, and the 7 Nov suspension truncated the control four weeks in. This closes the "nobody reports the ones that did not move" selection worry: the quiet end now has an independent licensed source behind it. Only percentage changes are cited; raw price points are not reproduced.
