Figure 2 · The two waves of sedimentation (H1)

A gallium circuit is not a free byproduct of an alumina refinery. It is a decision that can wait twenty years, or never happen

44 Chinese alumina refineries (sampling frame: all plants with capacity ≥0.8 Mt/yr, closures included). X = year the host refinery started up; Y = year its gallium circuit started up. On the diagonal = built with gallium from day one; the higher above the diagonal, the longer the retrofit waited.
Wave 1 lines (≤2014, hardened) Wave 2 lines (≥2020, ongoing) No gallium line to date Line status unknown vertical bar = start-up year known only as a range
Reading: 2015–2019 is an empty horizontal band. Among all dated gallium lines, not one started in those five years, exactly when gallium bottomed at $124–188/kg. Before the gap sits wave 1, which fixed what China held when the July 2023 export controls landed; after it comes wave 2, gathering pace as prices recovered from 2021 and the controls themselves lit the map. Vertical distance = years of lag. Luoyang Xiangjiang Wanji built its refinery in 2005 and its gallium line in 2025, a 20-year lag; its environmental filing states the plant adds 60 t/yr of byproduct gallium while alumina capacity stays unchanged. Same plant, alumina flat, gallium from zero: the most direct rebuttal to the idea that the circuit comes automatically with the refinery.
Sampling frame: roughly 40 refineries (38–42) nationwide with capacity ≥0.8 Mt/yr, covering over 95% of built capacity (~112 Mt, Mysteel 2025-06). The frame is triangulated from three independent sources: the SHFE alumina futures registered-brand list (2024), a 2019 plant-by-plant survey in China Nonferrous Metals News, and the 2023 CIB Futures / 2025 Founder CIFCO (Mysteel) capacity tables. The 44 rows here cover that frame, including the closed Nanchuan Xianfeng, Lushan Huiyuan (in bankruptcy restructuring), and plants outside the seven main provinces. 16 plants have unknown line status (no evidence found, which is not evidence of absence); they are kept off the main panel and shown only as a rug of host years — so this figure argues existence, not proportions. Facility-level exit does happen: Fangyuan's Lushan and Dengfeng lines were idle before 2019 while their hosts went through bankruptcy or equity sales — yet national capacity (Figure 1) never fell. Sedimentation is irreversible at the aggregate, not grain by grain.
Sources: host start-up years from company websites, China Nonferrous Metals News, local-government notices, and MEE filings; gallium-line years from environmental-impact disclosures, listed-company announcements and investor-Q&A replies, Sunresin's inquiry-letter response, Chalco's website, and trade media. Cell-level sources in host_vintage_gallium.csv. Where only "before year X" evidence exists, a vertical bar marks the range.
