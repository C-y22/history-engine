# The History Engine — flagship paper skeleton

Version 2, 2026-08-16 (v1 was 2026-08-12; all numbers here are on the completed-pool, corrected-diamond basis). Target length: 12,000–15,000 words of main text; 4 figures, 3 tables; appendices extra.

**Working titles** (choose at final draft)

1. *Sleeping Power: Latent Chokepoints in Global Supply Chains and How to Find Them Before They Wake*
2. *The History Engine: Assessing Supply-Chain Chokepoints from Criticality, Visibility, and Sunk Capital*
3. *Latent Leverage: How Supply-Chain Chokepoints Form Unseen — and Where They Will Surface Next*

**The claim in one sentence.** Supply-chain power sleeps in the latent zone — critical but unwatched. A sedimentation mechanism manufactures that zone (H1); public data can map it years in advance (H2a, an assessment test, not a policy prediction); and the intensity of an awakening follows criticality × unpreparedness (H2b).

## §1 Introduction (~3 pages)

- **Opening hook**: neon, 2022 — told in one paragraph and then dropped (the Mariupol purification plant: not scarce, simply unwatched; power activates the moment visibility flips).
- The puzzle: why chokepoints of this kind are always "discovered" after they detonate, and why the standard answers — comparative advantage, existing risk indices — cannot see them.
- Preview of the answer: three-axis framework → the latent zone → six showdowns as exams (exam A's top ten contains nine of the ten commodities later restricted, 21–27 months ahead of the event, p = 7.4e-06) → awakening intensity (dysprosium ≈ ×3 and yttrium +4,400% at one end; molybdenum ≈ 0 at the other).
- **Contributions** (four, one sentence each): (i) the three-axis framework and its "one axis, one treatment" operationalization; (ii) cognitive latency plus power = criticality × the visibility flip; (iii) the sedimentation / by-product theorem as mechanism; (iv) the back-test methodology and the NEON public benchmark.
- One roadmap paragraph.

## §2 Theory: three axes and time (~6 pages)

- **2.1 The three axes.** Reflexivity (can it be predicted → a design rule, in dialogue with Soros, Lucas, Grunberg & Modigliani 1954, Perdomo 2025, stated half a grade down); visibility (is anyone watching → self-measured; Farrell & Newman upgraded from anecdote to a node-level variable, cognitive latency contrasted with strategic latency); criticality (does it hurt → imported, Graedel / Nassar & Fortier, borrowed explicitly and not claimed as a contribution).
- **2.2 State space.** The theoretical four quadrants (reflexivity × visibility: latent zone / landmark zone / arena / undercurrent) — **Figure T**. The full eight-cell naming, each high-criticality corner with its low-criticality shadow, goes in an appendix box.
- **2.3 Time.** Hardening (yesterday's reflexive layer becomes today's non-reflexive one; Hughes on momentum, Berger & Luckmann); the power formula and activation; erosion and the prediction window (the honest clause: the window is the period in which erosion is not yet complete). The framework predicts the structural layer only — capacity and circuits — never prices or the timing of policy. **Structure, once in place, does not change easily; that is what makes advance assessment possible.**
- **2.4 The sedimentation mechanism (the theory behind H1).** "Physics supplies the arrow, history supplies the node"; the by-product theorem and its outsourced-circuit corollary; the Bayan Obo / Fogel counterfactual paragraph connecting to the cliometric lineage.

## §3 Research Design (~4 pages) — **write this first**

- The pool: the full USGS commodity list, 100 rows (86 + 14 lanthanides), externally defined. **The pool is now complete**: 88 rows valued, 11 documented-NA (USGS states no data is published), 15 REE rows on a single table; all 22 restricted commodities are scorable. No eligibility threshold; the 2022 critical-list gate survives only as a one-line robustness check.
- Visibility measurement: Media Cloud context-filtered counts (lineage: BBD, Fang & Peress), Wikipedia as a robustness ruler, and full disclosure of the single-corpus limitation and the term-map lock.
- Criticality, two specifications: "who should be afraid" (USGS supply-risk score) versus "who is being aimed at" (China's production share, measured at the processing stage where the restricted product is made — a rule codified after the fact and disclosed as such).
- The latent score and its normalization (three variants tested; conclusions unchanged).
- The six exams: the answer key (complete-set audit, 2026-08-12), cutoff-date discipline, the conservative cutoffs at D and E1, and the two safeguards. **Discipline of statement: this validates a map, not a prediction of sanctions — the joint proposition is stated explicitly.**
- H2b design: dependent variables are the one-month offshore price change and the onshore–offshore gap; selection-bias discipline (weak-response points are collected, not dropped).
- **Table 1**: the six exams (document number, dates, positives, cutoff).

## §4 Mechanism evidence: sedimentation (H1, ~5 pages)

- **Figure 1, sedimentation evidence**, three panels: hosts ×19; capacity that does not retreat in 21 years (price −67%, output −61%, capacity unmoved); price on a single basis. Ratchet / hysteresis wording.
- **Figure 2, the two waves**: the 44-plant sampling frame (three external sources define the frame); two waves with an empty 2015–19 band; Xiangjiang Wanji's 20-year lag ("60 tonnes of gallium added, alumina unchanged"); the Wenshan timeline of three statements.
- The outsourced circuit (Fangyuan's six bases; Weiqiao's legal-entity separation; fixed costs externalized → the line becomes nearly free to install → free to keep → the ratchet closes).
- Three confirming negatives (Tiangui, Zhongzhou, Longkou Donghai) plus the facility-level exit honesty clause (Lushan/Dengfeng line stoppages, Nanchuan closure — irreversible in aggregate is not the same as every grain being fixed).
- **The H1 → H2 bridge**: accumulating leverage generates no news, so high criticality with low visibility is an equilibrium product — the latent zone is continuously manufactured.
- Chinese-source moat on display: the Lanxiao inquiry letter and the emissions-fingerprint method (with its boundary: a positive is strong evidence, a negative is not proof of no gallium; legal-entity splits).
- Scope conditions: a single-commodity mechanism case (nested analysis, Lieberman 2005); generality is carried by H2's full pool; one sentence on the 95/5 source structure (USGS MYB 2022).

## §5 Revelation test (H2a, ~5 pages)

- **Figure 3**: the empirical four quadrants (visibility × criticality, restricted commodities marked) or the rank-trajectory chart (six exams × elements, moment of control marked) — one is the main figure, the other goes to the appendix.
- **Table 2**, main results: per-exam top-ten hits with hypergeometric p, element level and cluster level side by side. Element level: A 9/10 (p = 7.4e-06), B 9/10 (3.2e-06), C 9/10 (2.0e-06), D 10/10 (1.6e-08), E1 10/10 (2.0e-10), and at E2 the six positives sweep ranks 1–6 (1.3e-06). Cluster level: A 6/10 (4.4e-04), B 4/10 (**p = 0.019, cite exactly**), C 5/10 (9.5e-04), D 5/10 (4.1e-04); E1 and E2 degrade and are reported as they are.
- **Wollastonite is the single false light on the record** — a 75%-share filler mineral on no critical list. Removing the eligibility gate lit exactly one false lamp; that is the strongest internal evidence that the criticality instrument cannot be dropped. It forms a betting pair with fluorspar.
- The shackles, stated plainly: the after-the-fact codification of the criticality rule (rule plus disclosure); the cluster fold (both numbers reported); discriminability inside the rare earths (neodymium, praseodymium, lanthanum and cerium neither entered a top ten nor were restricted).
- Ablation and baselines, all on the same matched 88 rows: share-precise alone scores 10/10, **one better than the main specification — reported as-is**; the multiplicative score's value in H2a is restated as ranking quality (diamond at #7 versus #13) and robustness to coarse data (7/10 versus 5/10); invisibility alone 4/10; USGS score alone 2/10; a no-rare-earth variant still gives 5/10 (p = 3.3e-03). Factor separation is settled by H2b, not here — the collinearity section is pure disclosure.
- A direct answer to the "this is obvious" objection (three points: ranking inside the rare earths; the non-rare-earth obscurities; the fact that an explicit "obviousness" algorithm loses).
- Diagnosable failures: graphite (not latent — a tightening exam), molybdenum (a weak card), lanthanum and cerium (two readings, both reported).

## §6 Activation test (H2b, ~3 pages)

- **Figure 4**: pre-event latent power versus the one-month offshore price change, product specification, ρ = 0.89 (n = 10, p = 0.0008); over the longest window ρ = 0.80 (n = 12, p = 0.002). The formula is validated directly in its home domain.
- **Figure 5**: the division of labour across the three thermometers. News tracks unpreparedness alone (ρ = 0.66, p = 0.0005, n = 22; share alone 0.09 n.s.; the product 0.36, **marginal at p = 0.051 — never called significant**). Prices track the full product. Stocks sit in between (ρ = 0.54, p = 0.004).
- Two instrument calibrations, kept in a methods note rather than the headline: the placebo (targets ×12.9 versus ×1.26) and dark-versus-dark (×23.2 versus ×1.03). Quadrant contrast among the restricted: ×23.2 versus ×4.8 (n = 17/5, p = 0.0065).
- The stock case pair: Yunnan Germanium's three limit-ups against Hunan Gold closing under +2% — the market pays only for power it did not expect.
- Two exhibits worth their own paragraphs: antimony as the negative-space exhibit, and **synthetic diamond as the ceiling counterexample for the attention thermometer** — zero coverage in the 30 days after the announcement even under the corrected search term, a real zero rather than a vocabulary artifact; only the stock market read it (+25%).
- Method and bias handling: what the onshore–offshore split means (the gap is the power reading); completeness of the event set (all six); weak responses collected deliberately.

## §7 Prediction (1 page — the publication date is the notarization)

- Two products: China's likely next candidates (fluorspar first; cerium, lanthanum, magnesium, vanadium; neodymium and praseodymium as the largest card still unplayed) and a global latent list (hafnium, niobium, barite, tantalum).

## §8 Discussion (~3 pages)

- Scope conditions: a single actor, the mineral-commodity domain, and back-testing is not causal identification.
- Reflexive erosion on the record: the second retrofit wave, rising pre-exam visibility exam by exam, and publishing this paper as a quadrant-1-to-2 relocation operation (the engine is itself a reflexive machine).
- Half a page of generality, three examples: neon, hurricane-hit quartz, AI-datacentre inputs (bromine, GOES).
- One paragraph on the policy and commercial interface (Altana, Exiger — blind by construction to the by-product layer).
- Limitations: the term-map lock and its rule-(d) correction; Media Cloud as a single corpus (the construct is Western attention); inferred shares for the heavy rare earths Ho–Lu (capping them at 85% leaves 9 of 10 exam-A picks unchanged); the industry-sourced diamond share (conclusions hold anywhere from 68% to 99%).
