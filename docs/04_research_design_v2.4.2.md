## The claim (one sentence)

Supply-chain power sleeps in a latent zone of critical-but-invisible commodities; a sedimentation mechanism builds it (H1), public data can draw its map and the map held up against six reveals (H2a · assessment), and awakening intensity = criticality × unpreparedness (H2b · formula, validated).
